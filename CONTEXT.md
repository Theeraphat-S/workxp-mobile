# WorkXP (Pocket Quest)

WorkXP is an RPG gamified personal productivity mobile app that converts real-world tasks, habits, and daily routines into character progression, in-game rewards, and interactive statistics.

## Language

### Core Entities

**Hero**:
The user's RPG persona, holding stats, level, unlocked cosmetic gear, and visual attributes.
_Avoid_: User profile, player account, character sheet

**Quest**:
A discrete, one-off task with priority and deadline that grants EXP and stat points upon completion.
_Avoid_: To-do, task, chore, ticket

**Daily Quest**:
A recurring scheduled task that must be completed within a 24-hour cycle to maintain Streaks.
_Avoid_: Daily to-do, recurring task, routine

**Habit**:
A repeatable positive or negative behavioral action tracking frequency and granting Coins.
_Avoid_: Habit tracker, routine item

**Streak**:
The count of consecutive days a Hero has fulfilled their Daily Quests.
_Avoid_: Chain, consecutive days

### Economy & Progression

**EXP (Experience Points)**:
The non-spendable progression metric that drives Hero Leveling and unlocks cosmetic tiers.
_Avoid_: Score, points, XP

**Coin**:
The spendable in-game currency earned from completing habits and quests, used to purchase avatars, titles, and themes in the Shop.
_Avoid_: Gold, credits, money, gems

**Achievement**:
A permanent milestone trophy awarded for specific lifetime feats (e.g., 30-day streak, 100 quests completed).
_Avoid_: Badge, award, medal
